## Brand & Style

This design system is engineered for industrial heavy machinery distribution, fleet telemetry, and commercial field service operations. It bridges heavy-duty yellow iron durability with high-precision IoT fleet management intelligence.

The visual style blends **High-Contrast Industrial High-Tech** with **Refined Telemetry Glassmorphism**:
- Deep obsidian and charcoal foundation surfaces anchor the interface, evoking heavy cast iron, vulcanized rubber, and high-density industrial control consoles.
- Vibrant, hazard-grade amber and JCB-inspired gold function as mission-critical beacons, directing contractor and fleet manager actions with immediate clarity.
- Crisp, low-reflectance structural slate borders provide structural integrity and modular segmentation reminiscent of military-grade ruggedized field tablets.
- Real-time diagnostic readouts, IoT status monitors, and fleet estimators balance rugged utility with cutting-edge digital oversight.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritarian dark environment engineered to minimize eye strain in high-glare field environments and command centers.

### Hierarchy & Functional Roles
- **Primary Industrial Gold (`#FFB800`)**: Reserved for high-value contractor actions, purchase quotes, primary CTAs, telemetry warnings, and highlighted metric readouts. Never applied to bulk body text.
- **Structural Obsidian & Charcoal (`#0B0D10`, `#14171D`, `#1A1F26`)**: Forms the primary canvas and layered component surfaces. This provides physical weight and depth while eliminating uncalibrated light bleed.
- **Cool Slate Framework (`#262B35`, `#2E3646`)**: Defines component perimeters, technical dividers, and data tables with architectural precision.
- **Telemetry Emerald (`#10B981`)**: Dedicated exclusively to active system health, LiveLink ping status, operating indicators, and positive economic metrics.
- **Alert Crimson (`#EF4444`)**: Deployed strictly for mechanical faults, hydraulic over-pressure alerts, and critical maintenance windows.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements an engineering-grade layout designed for rapid data extraction under pressurized field conditions:

- **Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Technical, mechanical, and structurally confident. Display titles feature tight letter-spacing and uppercase styling for primary heavy machinery nomenclature (e.g., `RETROEXCAVADORA JCB 3CX ECO 4X4`).
- **Body (`Inter`)**: High-legibility neutral workhorse engineered for operational guides, machinery spec sheets, rental conditions, and multi-paragraph service dispatches.
- **Telematics & Data Readouts (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Strict tabular monospace numbers ensure diagnostic values (hydraulic bar pressures, machine hours, tachometer RPMs, and operational pricing) maintain vertical column alignment without visual jitter during live updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on a 12-column dynamic grid configured for dense telemetry visualization and streamlined contractor actions.

### Form Factors & Adaptation
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and a max content container width of 1360px. Side-by-side splits dedicate 60% of viewport width to interactive machinery configurations/telemetry graphs and 40% to sticky calculation and booking sidebars.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with 16px gutters and 24px margins. Diagnostic telemetry panels collapse from quad-grids into dual-column cards; interactive cost estimation calculators re-order beneath asset specifications.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with 12px gutters and 16px outer margins. Data-dense monitors transition to swipeable carousel segments or compact stacked rows with enlarged touch targets (minimum 48px height) suited for gloved or on-site handling.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through physical surface stratification combined with industrial backlighting rather than soft consumer shadows.

- **Baseplate (Level 0)**: Ground canvas rendered in `#0B0D10`. No elevation or backdrop filter.
- **Structural Tray (Level 1)**: Primary cards and module housings set in `#14171D` with a crisp 1px solid border in `#222733`.
- **Telemetry Float (Level 2)**: Real-time sensor pods, diagnostic popovers, and equipment selectors use `#1A1F26` at 85% opacity, paired with an 8px backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) and a 1px perimeter border in `#2E3646`.
- **Active State Illumination**: Interactive focus elements, active gauges, and primary buttons introduce an industrial safety under-glow: `0 0 20px rgba(255, 184, 0, 0.18)` for equipment actions and `0 0 16px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.2)` for active telemetry states.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft-Edged Industrial Geometry** (`roundedness: 1`). 

- Corners use a calculated radius of 4px (`0.25rem`) on buttons, metric pills, input fields, and telemetry meters, ascending to 8px (`0.5rem`) on major containment enclosures.
- Completely rounded pills and organic circular bubbles are strictly prohibited, preserving the visual identity of CNC-machined steel components and rugged instrument consoles.
- Precision chamfers or subtle technical accent notches (45-degree micro-corners) are permitted on high-level operational badges and live telemetry status tags.

## Components

### Action Buttons
- **Primary Machinery CTA**: Background in `#FFB800` with bold text in `#0B0D10`. Padding: `14px 24px`. Corner radius: `4px`. Subtle transition to `#E5A500` on hover with an ambient golden perimeter glow.
- **Secondary Telemetry / Spec CTA**: Translucent obsidian surface (`#14171D`) with a 1px structural border in `#2E3646` and off-white text (`#F8FAFC`). Expands to `#222733` on hover.
- **Emergency / Field Assist**: Deep obsidian core wrapped in an alert amber border (`#FFB800`), integrating a flashing status indicator.

### Telemetry & LiveLink Badges
- Micro-monitors formatted with `JetBrains Mono` at 11px uppercase.
- Status dots incorporate a continuous SVG pulse animation (`#10B981` for active connection, `#EF4444` for system fault). Encased in a frosted slate container with 1px border and 4px padding.

### Cards & Telemetry Containers
- Fabricated with deep charcoal panels (`#14171D`), structural slate outlines (`#222733`), and 20px internal padding.
- Metric cards showcase stacked data architecture: uppercase technical label on top, high-visibility monospace measurement in the center, and a progress micro-bar or delta marker at the bottom.

### Fleet Cost Estimator Widgets
- Dynamic interactive sliders using an obsidian track (`#222733`) with a `#FFB800` active fill bar and a 16px square knurled thumb.
- Dual-currency toggle switches (USD / DOP) featuring solid slate toggles and active amber text highlights.

### Forms & VIN Query Inputs
- Dark recessed background (`#0B0D10`) with a 1px border in `#2E3646`. 
- Focused inputs shift border color to `#FFB800` accompanied by a 2px inner shadow. Monospaced font rendering for tracking numbers, chassis VINs, and serial IDs.

### Technical Spec Tables
- Alternating row zebra-fills between `#14171D` and `#11141A`. Dividers set to 1px `#1E232B`.
- Key machine attributes left-aligned in neutral slate; metric values right-aligned in tabular monospace text for rapid cross-spec scanning.
